## Case Study: Chino Cafe

Chino Cafe is a Nigerian indigenous startup owned by Mark, a former banker who recently quit his job to pursue his passion for owning a business.

As the sole proprietor of the cafe, Mark personally manages the coffee brewing process while also overseeing the operations of the business. To support daily sales activities, he hired a sales assistant from Lagos to help attend to customers and record transactions.

Due to his banking background, Mark understands the importance of technology and data-driven decision-making. His goal is to use data effectively to grow Chino Cafe into a well-known coffee brand across Lagos.

The cafe currently sells the following products:

1. Cappuccino
2. Espresso
3. Latte

To keep operations simple for now, the sales assistant is responsible for recording:

1. Who bought the product
2. What product was purchased
3. Who handled the sale
4. The amount paid

---
Throughout this bootcamp, we will use this business scenario to learn how SQL is applied in real-world business environments.

Although this is a simplified example, it is similar to the kind of data problems data analysts and engineers solve in actual organizations. In a fully developed business, the database structure and operations would be much more complex.
